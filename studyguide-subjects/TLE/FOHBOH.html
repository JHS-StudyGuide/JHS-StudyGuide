Front of the House & Back of the House Operations

Understanding the essential division between guest-facing departments and backstage operational support in hotel management.
Front of the House (FOH) All hotel areas where team members directly interact with guests to deliver a memorable experience.
Back of the House (BOH) The essential backstage crew ensuring operational efficiency and seamless guest experiences behind the scenes.

1. Front of the House (FOH)

Front of the House includes all hotel areas where staff directly connect and interact with guests throughout their stay.

Direct Guest Service

Reception or Front Desk

  • The heart of the guest experience.
  • Manages check-in and checkout, payments, and room assignments.
Personal Assistance

Concierge Desk

  • Acts as a personal assistant for the guest.
  • Suggests local attractions, books dinner reservations, and arranges transportation.
First Impressions

Bell Services & Uniformed Staff

  • Includes bell attendants, doormen, and valet attendants.
  • Offers a welcoming presence and is often the first face guests see at a hotel.
Ambience & Brand

Lobby and Lounges

  • Sets the overall mood and reflects the hotel's brand identity.
  • Designed specifically for comfort, relaxation, and visual impact.
Guest Care

Guest Relations & VIP Services

  • Handles special requests, inquiries, complaints, and VIP guests.
  • Ensures guest concerns are resolved quickly and effectively.

2. Back of the House (BOH)

Back of the House acts as the backstage crew of the hospitality world, ensuring everything runs smoothly behind the scenes.

Corporate & Admin

Administration & Management Offices

  • Handles staffing, human resources (HR), training, payroll, accounting, marketing, and budgeting.
Bookings

Reservations Office / Call Center

  • Confirms bookings, answers telephone and email inquiries, and updates room availability.
Facility Maintenance

Engineering & Maintenance

  • Ensures that all physical building systems are functioning properly.
  • Responds quickly to repair broken items or issues in guest rooms.
Technology

IT and Systems Support

  • Manages Wi-Fi networks, hotel management software, electronic keycard systems, surveillance, and guest mobile apps.
Cleanliness & Logistics

Housekeeping Headquarters

  • Plans cleaning schedules, manages laundry inventory, and coordinates staff for guest room sanitation.
Linen Management

Laundry Room / Linen Room

  • Cleans thousands of linens daily.
  • In large hotels, industrial washers and dryers operate up to 18 hours a day.
Safety

Security & Surveillance Room

  • Monitors CCTV cameras, manages access control systems, and ensures complete guest and staff safety.
Supply Chain

Purchasing & Receiving Area

  • Manages the ordering, inventory tracking, and physical receiving of hotel supplies.
