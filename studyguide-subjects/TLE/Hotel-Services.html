2. Types of Hotels

Hotels are usually classified by who owns them and how they're run.

a

Independent Hotels

Privately owned and managed hotels that are not part of a group or brand.

b

Chain Hotels

Part of a group of hotels under one brand name, often with the same look, feel, and standards.

c

Franchise Hotels

Owned by an individual or group but operated under a well-known brand name, by paying fees to the brand owner.

d

Boutique Hotels

Small, stylish, and unique properties known for personalized service and creative design.

e

Resort Hotels

Usually located in tourist destinations, offering complete vacation experiences.
