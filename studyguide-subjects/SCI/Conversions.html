Conversion of Units & Physical Quantities

Comprehensive breakdown of fundamental quantities, SI/Metric conversions, English system equivalents, and measurement definitions.

1. Physical Quantities

Core measurements, definitions, and standard units in chemical science.

01 $\text{kg}$

Mass

Definition: Amount of matter in an object.

Behavior: Does not change with location.
02 $\text{N}$

Weight

Definition: Pull exerted by gravity on an object.

Behavior: Depends on mass & distance from the center of the planet (changes).
03 $\text{m}$

Length

Definition: Measurement of the extent of something along its greatest dimension.

04 $\text{km}$

Distance

Definition: Length of the path an object takes without regard for starting/ending place or direction.

05 $\text{m}^3$

Volume

Definition: How much space an object occupies.

06 $\text{atm / Pa}$

Pressure

Definition: Force applied perpendicular to the surface per unit area.

07 $\text{K / °C / °F}$

Temperature

Definition: Measure of average kinetic energy of the particles in an object.

08 $\text{g/cm}^3$

Density

Definition: Ratio of the space taken up (volume) relative to the amount of matter (mass).

2. Common Equations (SI System)

Standard metric system conversion factors categorized by dimensional type.

Length Equivalents

  • $1\text{ km}$ = $1,000\text{ m}$
  • $1\text{ m}$ = $100\text{ cm}$
  • $1\text{ m}$ = $1,000\text{ mm}$
  • $1\text{ cm}$ = $10\text{ mm}$
  • $1\text{ m}$ = $3.281\text{ ft}$

Mass Equivalents

  • $1\text{ kg}$ = $1,000\text{ g}$
  • $1\text{ g}$ = $1,000\text{ mg}$
  • $1\text{ mg}$ = $1,000\text{ µg}$ (microgram)

Volume Equivalents

  • $1\text{ L}$ = $10\text{ dL}$
  • $1\text{ L}$ = $1,000\text{ mL}$
  • $1\text{ L}$ = $1,000,000\text{ µL}$
  • $1\text{ dL}$ = $100\text{ mL}$
  • $1\text{ mL}$ = $1\text{ cm}^3 = 1\text{ cc}$

3. Common Equations (English System)

Standard Imperial/English conversion factors categorized by dimensional type.

Length Equivalents

  • $1\text{ ft}$ = $12\text{ in}$
  • $1\text{ yd}$ = $3\text{ ft}$
  • $1\text{ mi}$ = $5,280\text{ ft}$
  • $1\text{ mi}$ = $1.61\text{ km}$

Mass Equivalents

  • $1\text{ lb}$ = $16\text{ oz}$
  • $1\text{ ton}$ = $2,000\text{ lb}$
  • $1\text{ oz}$ = $28.35\text{ g}$

Volume Equivalents

  • $1\text{ qt}$ = $4\text{ cups}$
  • $1\text{ qt}$ = $2\text{ pints}$
  • $1\text{ qt}$ = $32\text{ fl oz}$
  • $1\text{ gal}$ = $4\text{ qt}$

4. Quick Reference Summary Table

Quantity Description / Behavior Standard Unit(s)
Mass Constant amount of matter in a body $\text{kg, g, mg, lb, oz}$
Weight Variable force due to gravitational pull $\text{N}$ (Newton)
Length / Distance Spatial extent or continuous path traveled $\text{m, km, cm, mm, ft, in, yd, mi}$
Volume Three-dimensional space occupied $\text{m}^3\text{, L, dL, mL, cc, qt, gal}$
Pressure Perpendicular force per surface area $\text{atm, Pa}$
Temperature Average kinetic energy of particles $\text{K, °C, °F}$
Density Ratio of mass to volume ($\frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$) $\text{g/cm}^3$
