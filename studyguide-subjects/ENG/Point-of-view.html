3. Impact of Point of View & Perspective

How perspective alters storytelling, builds empathy, and reflects human culture.

1. The Impact of POV

  • Shapes Empathy: POV directly shapes the reader's empathy.
  • Determines Loyalty: It determines who we root for and who we fear.
  • Meaning Shift: A change in POV can completely flip the meaning of a story.

2. The Antagonist's Perspective

  • Villain's Tale: What if the 'villain' told the story?
  • Example: Viewing Cinderella from the stepmother's POV.
  • Core Question: How does the story change when told from another side?

3. POV and Cultural Value

  • Understanding Communities: POV helps us understand different cultures and communities.
  • Challenging Biases: It challenges our biases by forcing us into another's shoes.
  • Shared Human Experience: It highlights shared human experience across different "lenses."

4. Narrative Techniques

Narrative techniques are tools writers use to tell a story in a clear, exciting, and meaningful way. They help make a story more interesting and help readers feel emotions.

Core Elements Included: Narrative Techniques encompass POV, Characterization, and Plot.
1

Flashback

Pauses what is happening right now to reveal events from the past.

2

Foreshadowing

A small hint provided by the author about something important that will happen later.

3

Dialogue

Characters talk, think, feel, and move the story forward through conversation.

4

Stream of Consciousness

Shows a character's continuous flow of thoughts in real time.

5

Suspense & Cliffhanger

Builds tension, anticipation, and leaves key conflicts temporarily unresolved.
