Target & Invasion Games

A comprehensive guide covering the different styles, examples, and playing methods for target and invasion games.

Target Games Throwing, sliding, or rolling objects to hit or get close to a target.
Invasion Games Invading opponents' space to score goals by throwing, sliding, or rolling objects.
Styles of Play Methods for defending area and actively invading opposite side.

2. Invasion Games

Different styles, player interactions, and methods for playing invasion games.

1. Invasion Games Overview

  • Objective: Score goals by throwing, sliding, or rolling an object.
  • Opponent Interaction: Opponents are allowed to invade space or area.
  • Timing: Both teams play at the same time.
  • Examples: Football, Soccer, Basketball.

Styles of Play

Defensive

  • Approach: Opponent stays on their side, not invading opposite side.
  • Purpose: Defending to disallow enemy entry into theirs.

Offensive

  • Approach: Opponent actively tries to invade opponents' side.
  • Purpose: Intending to steal goal from their opponents' side.
