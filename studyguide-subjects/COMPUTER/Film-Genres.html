Film Genres & Types

What a film genre is, why genres matter, and a quick-reference guide to the ten most common types.

1. What Is a Film Genre?

A category or type of movie built around a specific style, theme, or set of characteristics — it tells you upfront what kind of story or emotion to expect.

A film genre groups movies that share similar storytelling patterns, tone, or subject matter (think: chases and explosions for Action, or jump-scares for Horror). Genres exist because they're useful — for the people making films, the people watching them, and the systems that sort them.

2. Types of Genres

Ten genres you'll see referenced most often, with the one-line giveaway that identifies each.

Action

Fast-paced, with stunts, chases, and explosions.

Comedy

Built to make the audience laugh through jokes or silly situations.

Drama

Realistic, character-driven emotional stories.

Horror

Designed to scare or shock with suspense, monsters, or ghosts.

Sci-Fi

Futuristic tech, space, aliens, or time travel.

Fantasy

Magical worlds, mythical creatures, imaginary settings.

Romance

Centers on love stories and relationships.

Adventure

Exciting journeys or quests, often in exotic locations.

Documentary

Non-fiction — real events, people, or facts.

Musical

Characters express emotion through songs and dance.
